# Vintora Code of Conduct

> **Prototype mode — no real orders are placed. NO-GO for public launch.**

## Our standard

Be respectful, precise and constructive. Assume good intent, but hold claims to evidence. Give credit for others' work, respect review boundaries, and do not pressure a reviewer to waive a security, legal or launch gate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, personal data or unlicensed material in issues, PRs or discussions.
- Misrepresenting prototype output as real orders, verified inventory, regulatory approval or production operation.
- Retaliation against a good-faith security report or conduct complaint.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the same private maintainer channel listed in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md). Security vulnerabilities must use that private path, never a public issue. Include the affected interaction, dates, participants and the outcome you seek. Reports are acknowledged within 5 business days, reviewed by a maintainer who is not the subject of the complaint, and kept confidential to the extent permitted by law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation or block a contributor. Enforcement decisions are recorded by role, not by public disclosure of personal details. Appeals are reviewed by a second maintainer who was not involved in the original decision. No enforcement action changes the repository's NO-GO posture or evidence requirements.

This conduct policy is a prototype governance definition; no hosted moderation process or named individual is attached to this workspace.
