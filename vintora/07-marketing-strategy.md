## 1. Brand Positioning

### 1.1 Positioning statement

> **For** curious drinkers, trade professionals and producers who want certainty in a category built on opacity, **Vintora** is the AI-native global wine and spirits platform that turns a bottle into known provenance, a palate into a measurable taste profile, and a cellar into an intelligent asset. **Unlike** review apps that only rate what you already bought, marketplaces that only transact, or distributors that only ship, Vintora closes the loop: **taste profile → discovery → purchase → consumption → review → better profile**, with custody and compliance recorded at every step.

The line we defend: ***The World's Cellar, Intelligent.*** It carries three claims: **scale** (the world's cellar), **authority** (a curated cellar, not a catalogue dump), **intelligence** (the system learns). Every campaign ladders to one of those words.

### 1.2 Archetype, voice, visual identity

**Archetype:** Sage (primary), Magician (the AI that makes the right bottle appear), Caregiver (the restraint that keeps us responsible). Never the Jester or the seducer of excess.

**Voice:** (1) Precise, never pretentious — "high acidity, 12.5% ABV, serve at 10–12°C" beats "profound elegance." (2) Always explain reasoning — "matches your high-acid preference and your saved rating of the 2021 Chablis." (3) Confident about uncertainty — disclose unverified vintages. (4) Adult-to-adult: responsible drinking is a normal sentence, not a bolted-on warning. (5) One vocabulary globally; only language and currency localise.

| Visual hook | Execution rule |
|---|---|
| **The Cellar Grid** | Vertical bottle-ratio columns as layout scaffolding on web, packaging, decks and signage. |
| **Provenance Thread** | One continuous hairline tracing producer → vineyard → vintage → bottle → custody on product pages, invoices, Vault certificates and AR scans. |
| **Palette / type** | Cellar Ink `#171A1F`, Bordeaux `#6E1F2E`, Terracotta `#C0653F`, Parchment `#F4EFE6`, Proof Green `#2E6B57` (verified states only). Editorial serif for vintages, geometric sans for UI; tabular numerals. |
| **AI signature / photography** | Subtle reasoning trace on Sommelier and Assist answers, never anthropomorphised. Real bottles, cellars and people at work; no stock party imagery. |

**Pillars:** Palate & Pairing 35%; Provenance & Trust 20%; Value & Investing 15%; Trade Craft 15%; Culture & Responsible Enjoyment 15%.

**Rule:** no AI asset ships without a named human owner, review timestamp and source list in the CMS. That is our operational definition of "expert-reviewed."

**E-E-A-T / YMYL:** alcohol is treated as YMYL-adjacent (health, legal and financial implications); Vault is financial YMYL. Name credentialed authors and log reviews; cite producer sheets, appellation rules and custody records; display corrections and commercial disclosures; put risk, illiquidity and the full fee schedule next to every investment claim; never promise returns or imply health benefits; no content designed to appeal to minors (no cartoon, gaming or youth-celebrity cues). Age-gate where required and carry *"Please drink responsibly. Adults of legal drinking age only."*

## 8. Seller / Brand Acquisition (Supply Side)

**Targets:** 150 supplier MOUs by end 2026; 6k signed sellers 2027; 25k 2028; 60k 2029; 110k 2031; 250k 2034 (canon).

1. **Prospecting.** Score leads 0–100 on allocation scarcity, export readiness, digital maturity and wave-market fit. Sources: appellation registries, exhibitor lists, distributor catalogues, Vintora Market search gaps and sommelier referrals. BD works the top quartile first.
2. **Data-backed pitch.** Lead with demand evidence: "buyers in your three target markets searched your appellation 41k times last quarter; 8,400 saved a bottle within $5 of your price band." Then the operating case: one listing across Vintora Market and Vintora Trade, localised Vintora Studio content, Vintora Flow logistics, Vintora Seller payouts (Free → $99 → $399 → $999/mo + 0.5–2% processing).
3. **Signing incentives.** Six months free Seller ($399 tier), $500 Studio credits, first-month Flow insurance waived, founding-producer provenance badge. Cap $2,500 per seller in 2027; CFO approval above that.
4. **Co-marketing.** Producer-story content, creator visits, featured in-app placement, co-branded tasting kits, Rewards experiences. Sellers fund 50% of approved local activations from 2028.
5. **Exhibitions.** Prowein, Vinexpo and sommelier trade shows are the three anchors. Each has a pre-booked meeting list, live signup station, AR provenance demo and 14-day follow-up sprint. Target ≥300 qualified meetings and ≥120 MOUs per major show from 2027.
6. **Activation.** ≥70% of signed sellers live with ≥10 SKUs in 30 days; underperformers get a 21-day rescue playbook, then lose incentive obligations.

## 11. Compliance in Marketing

1. **Age gating.** Gate before Market entry and lead forms: 21+ in the US and other 21+ markets, 18+ elsewhere, with third-party verification at purchase. No targeting minors — no underage-seeded lookalikes, family-interest segments or youth-oriented placements.
2. **Platform policy.** Maintain Google/Meta alcohol certification and geo restrictions per market; treat TikTok alcohol ads as prohibited unless written policy says otherwise; respect YouTube in-stream limits; creator briefs bar visible consumption by anyone appearing under 25 and bar glamorising intake. Re-verify quarterly and log.
3. **Responsible drinking.** Standard line on all assets: *"Please drink responsibly. For adults of legal drinking age only."* No health claims, no impaired-driving tolerance, no "drink to fix a feeling" framing; Vintora Sommelier refuses unsafe-quantity queries.
4. **Jurisdictions.** France: Évin-style restrictions — editorial, producer-led and CRM dominate. US: state rules on shipping, DTC and coupons; no cross-state availability promises. UAE: tourism/tourist-status and emirate rules reviewed locally. LatAm: country tax/ad rules; no launch before licence clearance (`08-launch-roadmap.md` §12).
5. **Claims.** Provenance claims require a graph record. Vault content must disclose risk, illiquidity, fees and historical-not-forecast data; no guaranteed returns or "safe asset" language. Price claims must be reproducible in Market.
6. **Privacy.** GDPR/CCPA/LGPD/PIPL consent for tracking and CRM; anonymised insights only in Rewards reporting; self-exclusion propagates to all channels within 24 hours.
7. **Governance.** Three-step asset check: brand editor → alcohol-policy checker → jurisdictional counsel where required. CMO and Chief Compliance Officer own the quarterly audit; repeat agency violations mean termination.
