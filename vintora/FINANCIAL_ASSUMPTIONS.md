## Method definitions

- **users** — Registered accounts in the stated horizon; not monthly active buyers and not reported traction. Definition authority: `10-investor-pitch-deck.md Appendix A2`.
- **gmv** — Gross merchandise value before refunds, taxes, duties, fulfilment, payment cost and revenue share. Modelled as Users × Buyer conversion × Orders per buyer × AOV. Definition authority: `10-investor-pitch-deck.md Appendix A2; 09-growth-strategy.md growth tree`.
- **netRevenue** — Recognised platform revenue after seller payouts and agreed revenue share; not GMV and not cash collected. Definition authority: `10-investor-pitch-deck.md Appendix A2; 02-revenue-model.md section 3`.
- **takeRate** — Net revenue divided by GMV for the same horizon, including transactional and non-GMV revenue components where stated. Definition authority: `02-revenue-model.md section 3.1`.
- **cac** — Blended customer acquisition cost: total acquisition spend divided by newly acquired registered users in the modelled period. Definition authority: `07-marketing-strategy.md section 5; 10-investor-pitch-deck.md Appendix A2`.
- **aov** — Average order value: modelled GMV divided by modelled orders in the same horizon. Definition authority: `10-investor-pitch-deck.md Appendix A2`.
- **repeatPurchaseRate** — Share of registered users with a qualifying repeat purchase within the defined twelve-month measurement window. Definition authority: `09-growth-strategy.md KPI dashboard`.
- **contributionMargin** — Net revenue less variable fulfilment, payment, leakage and directly attributable acquisition cost, divided by net revenue. Definition authority: `02-revenue-model.md unit economics`.
- **enterpriseValue** — Illustrative multiple-based valuation range using Y8 revenue multiples; no cap table, audited accounts, market evidence or transaction is attached. Definition authority: `02-revenue-model.md section 8`.
