## Non-negotiable rules

1. No invented medical credentials or healthcare partnerships.
2. No fake testimonials or performance metrics; every number is labelled as a planning assumption unless it has an evidence record.
3. No unverified doctor may practise through the platform.
4. No unlicensed pharmacy operations.
5. No AI may independently diagnose or prescribe.
6. All patient information is protected by default.
7. Human approval is required for sensitive clinical and financial operations.
8. All development decisions are documented.
9. No coding until the owner reviews and approves the architecture and requirements.
10. Reference companies (Eka Care, Practo, Tata 1mg) are business-model archetypes only — no branding, software, design or IP is copied.
